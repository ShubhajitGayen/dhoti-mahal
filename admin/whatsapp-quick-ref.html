WhatsApp Quick Guide

📍 Where to Find It

Admin → Orders → Click any order → Scroll right panel

🎯 5 Quick Templates

✅ Order Confirmed
When you receive order

⚙️ Processing
Order being prepared

📦 Shipped
With tracking info

✓ Delivered
Order reached customer

💵 Payment Reminder
For pending payments

✍️ 3 Steps to Send

  1. Click template button
  2. Review message preview
  3. Click "OK" → WhatsApp opens → Send

💡 Pro Tips

  • Use "Confirmed" right after payment
  • Send "Shipped" same day as dispatch
  • Use "Custom Message" for special requests
  • Check "Open Chat" for quick replies
  • Keep messages short & friendly

⚠️ Troubleshooting

WhatsApp doesn't open? Check browser pop-up blocker
Message blank? Ensure customer phone is filled
Variable showing like {name}? Contact developer
